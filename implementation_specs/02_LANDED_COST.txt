NAVIFREIGHT — LANDED COST SPECIFICATION

Objective
Show the user the delivered commodity acquisition cost using the benchmark decision economics.

FORMULA
Landed Cost = Commodity FOB + Ocean Freight

Where:
Commodity FOB = procurement benchmark commodity price
Ocean Freight = forecast freight USD/t

UNIT DISCIPLINE
Iron ore:
- commodity price: USD/dmt
- freight: USD/t
- preserve the displayed unit convention consistently

Coal:
- commodity price: USD/mt
- freight: USD/t
- preserve the displayed unit convention consistently

Do not silently mix:
- different iron ore grades
- CFR and FOB prices
- unrelated coal benchmarks
- unrelated freight route assessments

DATA RULES
- Use World Bank Pink Sheet monthly commodity history as the standardized procurement benchmark.
- Latest published observation must be respected.
- New commodity history supports procurement only.
- It must NOT be appended to freight training data.

UI
Show:
- Commodity FOB benchmark
- Expected ocean freight
- Estimated landed cost
- Units
- Source/provenance where available

Do not invent:
- port charges
- demurrage
- insurance
- handling
- taxes
- inland transport
unless those are explicitly added later as separate modeled inputs.

TESTS
- formula correctness
- unit consistency
- missing-price handling
- missing-freight handling
- provenance preserved
